## 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the Governance Decision Register (GDR) for the NSS ERP project. It defines the principles, lifecycle, rules, and documentation requirements for recording governance decisions to ensure that governance activities remain transparent, traceable, consistent, and auditable throughout the project lifecycle.

The Governance Decision Register serves as the authoritative record of approved governance decisions and their associated rationale.

## 2. Scope

This standard applies to all governance decisions affecting:

- Governance principles.
- Governance standards.
- Authoritative references.
- Repository governance.
- Requirement traceability.
- Governance change control.
- Project governance processes.
- Governance exceptions.
- Other governance artifacts approved by the governance authority.

All governance decisions shall be recorded in accordance with this standard.

## 4. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Governance Decision | A formally approved determination that establishes, modifies, interprets, or retires a governance principle, standard, policy, or controlled governance artifact. |
| Governance Decision Register | The authoritative repository of approved governance decisions and their supporting information. |
| Decision Identifier | A unique and permanent identifier assigned to each governance decision. |
| Decision Authority | The individual or governance body authorized to approve governance decisions. |
| Decision Record | The documented information associated with a governance decision, including its rationale, approval, implementation status, and related artifacts. |
| Decision Lifecycle | The sequence of activities through which a governance decision progresses from proposal to retirement. |
| Decision Status | The current state of a governance decision within its lifecycle. |

## 5. Governance Decision Principles

The governance decision principles defined in this section establish the foundation for recording and managing governance decisions throughout the NSS ERP project. These principles ensure that governance decisions remain transparent, traceable, accountable, and consistently documented.

### 5.1 GDR-DEC-001 — Documented Governance Decisions

Every governance decision shall be formally documented before becoming effective.

Decision records shall clearly identify:

- The decision.
- The business or governance rationale.
- The approving authority.
- The approval date.
- The affected governance artifacts.

Undocumented governance decisions shall not be considered authoritative.

### 5.2 GDR-DEC-002 — Unique Decision Identity

Every governance decision shall be assigned a unique and permanent Decision Identifier.

Decision identifiers shall:

- Be assigned only once.
- Remain immutable throughout the decision lifecycle.
- Never be reused.
- Be referenced consistently across related governance artifacts.

### 5.3 GDR-DEC-003 — Decision Traceability

Governance decisions shall maintain complete traceability to the artifacts they affect.

Traceability shall include references to:

- Authoritative references.
- Governance standards.
- Change Requests.
- Requirement artifacts.
- Implementation artifacts.
- Related governance decisions.

Traceability relationships shall remain complete throughout the decision lifecycle.

### 5.4 GDR-DEC-004 — Governance Accountability (Decision-Level)

Every individual governance decision shall identify the responsible approving authority.

Decision records shall preserve accountability by documenting:

- The approving authority.
- The approval date.
- The implementation status.
- Related governance reviews.
- Any subsequent revisions or retirement.

This rule establishes accountability at the level of each individual governance decision. Governance accountability for the Governance Decision Register as a whole is separately established at the register level by GDR-DATA-005.

Governance accountability shall remain auditable throughout the decision lifecycle.

## 6. Governance Decision Lifecycle

This section defines the lifecycle of governance decisions within the NSS ERP project. The lifecycle establishes a controlled process for proposing, reviewing, approving, implementing, monitoring, and retiring governance decisions while ensuring transparency, accountability, and traceability throughout their existence.

### 6.1 Decision Proposal

Every governance decision shall originate from a formally documented proposal.

A decision proposal shall include:

- A unique proposal identifier.
- A clear description of the proposed decision.
- The business or governance justification.
- The affected governance artifacts.
- Supporting analysis, where applicable.
- The proposing individual or governance body.

Decision proposals shall be submitted for governance review before further processing.

### 6.2 Decision Review

Every proposed governance decision shall undergo a formal review.

The review shall evaluate:

- Alignment with governance principles.
- Consistency with authoritative references.
- Impact on existing governance standards.
- Risks and dependencies.
- Effects on project implementation and traceability.

Review findings shall be documented as part of the decision record.

### 6.3 Decision Approval

Governance decisions shall be approved only by the designated governance authority.

Approval shall confirm that:

- The decision proposal has been reviewed.
- The supporting analysis is complete.
- Governance objectives are satisfied.
- Risks are acceptable.
- Implementation is authorized.

Approved decisions shall become part of the Governance Decision Register.

### 6.4 Decision Implementation

Approved governance decisions shall be implemented in accordance with the approved governance change control process.

Implementation activities shall:

- Update affected governance artifacts.
- Maintain traceability relationships.
- Record implementation details.
- Preserve document version history.
- Notify relevant stakeholders, where required.

Implementation shall be completed in a controlled and auditable manner.

### 6.5 Decision Monitoring

Implemented governance decisions shall be monitored to verify their continued effectiveness.

Monitoring activities shall evaluate:

- Compliance with the approved decision.
- Achievement of intended governance outcomes.
- Emerging risks or issues.
- Need for corrective action or revision.

Monitoring results shall be documented as governance records.

### 6.6 Decision Retirement

Governance decisions that are no longer applicable shall be formally retired.

Decision retirement shall:

- Document the reason for retirement.
- Identify any replacement decisions.
- Preserve historical decision records.
- Maintain traceability to affected governance artifacts.

Retired decisions shall remain available for historical reference and audit purposes.

## 7. Governance Decision Rules

This section establishes the operational rules governing the creation, approval, implementation, maintenance, and retirement of governance decisions. These rules ensure that governance decisions remain authoritative, traceable, consistent, and auditable throughout their lifecycle.

### 7.1 Decision Identification

Every governance decision shall be assigned a unique and permanent Decision Identifier.

Decision identifiers shall:

- Be unique within the Governance Decision Register.
- Be assigned only once.
- Never be reused.
- Remain unchanged throughout the decision lifecycle.
- Be referenced consistently across all related governance artifacts.

Decision identifiers shall serve as the primary reference for governance traceability.

### 7.2 Decision Documentation

Every governance decision shall be supported by a complete Decision Record.

A Decision Record shall include:

- Decision identifier.
- Decision title.
- Decision description.
- Business or governance rationale.
- Decision status.
- Approval information.
- Related governance artifacts.
- Implementation status.
- References to supporting documentation, where applicable.

Decision records shall be maintained as controlled governance artifacts.

### 7.3 Decision Ownership

Every governance decision shall identify an accountable Decision Authority.

The Decision Authority shall be responsible for:

- Reviewing the proposed decision.
- Approving or rejecting the decision.
- Ensuring compliance with governance standards.
- Overseeing implementation.
- Authorizing subsequent revisions or retirement.

Decision ownership shall remain clearly documented throughout the decision lifecycle.

### 7.4 Decision Version Management

Governance decisions shall be managed using controlled version management practices.

Version management shall:

- Preserve historical decision records.
- Record revision history.
- Maintain decision identity.
- Document significant changes.
- Support governance auditability.

Previous versions shall remain available for historical reference.

### 7.5 Decision Traceability

Every governance decision shall maintain traceability to related governance artifacts.

Traceability relationships shall include, where applicable:

- Authoritative references.
- Governance standards.
- Governance Change Requests.
- Requirement artifacts.
- Solution artifacts.
- Implementation artifacts.
- Related governance decisions.

Traceability shall remain complete throughout the decision lifecycle.

### 7.6 Decision Review and Maintenance

Governance decisions shall be reviewed periodically to ensure continued relevance and effectiveness.

Reviews shall determine whether a decision should be:

- Retained without change.
- Revised.
- Superseded by another decision.
- Retired.

Review outcomes shall be documented within the Governance Decision Register.

## 8. Governance Decision Register Principles

The Governance Decision Register shall function as the authoritative repository for governance decisions within the NSS ERP project. These principles establish the architectural requirements for maintaining a complete, reliable, and auditable record of governance decisions.

### 8.1 GDR-DATA-001 — Single Authoritative Register

The Governance Decision Register shall serve as the single authoritative repository for all approved governance decisions.

No parallel or unofficial governance decision repositories shall be maintained.

### 8.2 GDR-DATA-002 — Immutable Decision Identity

Every governance decision shall retain its original Decision Identifier throughout its lifecycle.

Decision identifiers shall remain permanent and shall never be reassigned or reused.

### 8.3 GDR-DATA-003 — Complete Decision History

The Governance Decision Register shall preserve the complete historical record of every governance decision.

Historical records shall include:

- Initial approval.
- Subsequent revisions.
- Related governance changes.
- Retirement information, where applicable.

Historical information shall remain available for governance reviews and audits.

### 8.4 GDR-DATA-004 — Decision Traceability by Design

The Governance Decision Register shall support complete traceability between governance decisions and related project artifacts.

Traceability shall enable efficient governance reviews, impact analysis, compliance verification, and historical analysis.

### 8.5 GDR-DATA-005 — Governance Accountability (Register-Level)

The Governance Decision Register shall preserve governance accountability by recording decision ownership, approval authority, implementation status, and related governance activities.

This rule establishes accountability at the level of the Governance Decision Register as a whole — the register's obligation to preserve and expose accountability data across all decisions it holds. It is distinct from GDR-DEC-004, which establishes accountability for each individual governance decision.

Accountability information shall remain complete, accurate, and auditable throughout the decision lifecycle.

## 9. Governance Compliance Requirements

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all governance decisions recorded within the NSS ERP project. Compliance verification ensures that governance decisions are properly documented, approved, implemented, maintained, and retired in accordance with the established governance framework.

### 9.1 Compliance Verification

Governance compliance shall be verified throughout the decision lifecycle.

Verification activities shall confirm that:

- Governance decisions are properly documented.
- Decision identifiers are unique and permanent.
- Required reviews and approvals have been completed.
- Traceability relationships remain complete.
- Decision records are accurate and up to date.

Compliance verification shall be performed as part of governance reviews and release readiness assessments.

### 9.2 Non-Compliance Management

Instances of non-compliance shall be identified, documented, assessed, and resolved through the governance process.

Non-compliance management shall include:

- Identification of the non-compliance.
- Assessment of governance impact.
- Definition of corrective actions.
- Assignment of responsibility.
- Verification of corrective action completion.

Governance decisions shall not become effective until significant non-compliance issues have been resolved unless a formally approved governance exception exists.

### 9.3 Periodic Governance Review

The Governance Decision Register shall be reviewed periodically to ensure its continued accuracy, completeness, and effectiveness.

Periodic reviews shall evaluate:

- Decision completeness.
- Traceability integrity.
- Consistency across governance artifacts.
- Compliance with governance standards.
- Opportunities for governance improvement.

Review outcomes shall be documented and retained as governance records.

## Appendix A – Governance Decision Register Template

The Governance Decision Register shall maintain a controlled record of all approved governance decisions.

The minimum information recorded for each decision shall include:

| Field | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Decision ID | Unique decision identifier |
| Decision Title | Short descriptive title |
| Decision Description | Summary of the governance decision |
| Decision Rationale | Business or governance justification |
| Decision Authority | Approving authority |
| Approval Date | Date of approval |
| Decision Status | Proposed, Approved, Implemented, Superseded, Retired |
| Related Documents | References to affected governance artifacts |
| Related Change Request | Associated Change Request, where applicable |
| Implementation Status | Current implementation status |
| Review Date | Next scheduled review |
| Remarks | Additional information |
