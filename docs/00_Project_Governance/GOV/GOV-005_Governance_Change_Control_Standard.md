## 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the governance change control framework for the NSS ERP project. It defines the principles, processes, rules, and controls governing changes to governance documents, authoritative references, standards, policies, and related governance artifacts.

The objective of this standard is to ensure that governance changes are proposed, evaluated, approved, implemented, and documented in a controlled, transparent, and auditable manner while preserving the integrity and stability of the project governance framework.

## 2. Scope

This standard applies to:

- Governance standards
- Authoritative references
- Governance policies
- Governance procedures
- Governance principles
- Repository governance artifacts
- Traceability standards
- Governance decision records
- Controlled governance documentation

This standard is mandatory for all governance changes within the NSS ERP project.

## 4. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Governance Change | Any approved modification to governance documents, standards, policies, principles, procedures, or controlled governance artifacts. |
| Change Request | A formal proposal requesting the addition, modification, or retirement of a governance artifact. |
| Governance Baseline | The approved and controlled set of governance artifacts that define the current governance framework. |
| Impact Assessment | The assessment of the effects of a proposed governance change on related documents, processes, systems, and project artifacts. |
| Governance Approval | Formal authorization to implement a governance change following the prescribed review process. |
| Governance Exception | An approved deviation from one or more governance requirements under defined circumstances. |
| Change Record | The documented history of a governance change, including approvals, implementation, and related decisions. |

## 5. Governance Change Control Principles

The governance change control principles defined in this section establish the foundation for managing changes to the NSS ERP governance framework. These principles ensure that governance changes are controlled, transparent, auditable, and aligned with the project's governance objectives.

### 5.1 GOV-CHG-001 — Controlled Governance Evolution

Governance artifacts shall evolve only through approved governance change control procedures.

All governance changes shall be formally proposed, evaluated, approved, implemented, and documented before becoming effective.

### 5.2 GOV-CHG-002 — Governance Baseline Protection

The approved governance baseline shall remain protected from unauthorized modification.

Changes affecting the governance baseline shall be implemented only after successful completion of the governance change control process.

### 5.3 GOV-CHG-003 — Governance Impact Assessment

Every proposed governance change shall undergo an impact assessment before approval.

The assessment shall identify:

- Affected governance documents;
- Dependent project artifacts;
- Implementation implications;
- Traceability impacts; and
- Potential governance risks.

The results of the assessment shall be documented as part of the change record.

### 5.4 GOV-CHG-004 — Governance Decision Transparency

Governance decisions shall be transparent, traceable, and supported by documented rationale.

Approved governance changes shall be recorded in the Governance Decision Register to preserve decision history and accountability.

## 6. Governance Change Control Process

This section defines the controlled process for proposing, evaluating, approving, implementing, verifying, and closing governance changes within the NSS ERP project. The process ensures that governance changes are consistently managed, fully traceable, and appropriately authorized before becoming part of the approved governance baseline.

### 6.1 Change Initiation

Governance changes shall originate through a formal Change Request.

A Change Request shall:

- Clearly describe the proposed change.
- Identify the reason for the change.
- Reference the affected governance artifacts.
- Identify the requesting party.
- Include supporting documentation where applicable.

No governance change shall proceed without a formally documented Change Request.

### 6.2 Change Review

Every proposed governance change shall undergo a formal review before approval.

The review shall evaluate:

- Governance alignment.
- Business justification.
- Impact on existing governance standards.
- Impact on project artifacts.
- Potential risks and dependencies.

Review findings shall be documented as part of the Change Record.

### 6.3 Change Approval

Governance changes shall be approved only by the designated governance authority.

Approval shall confirm that:

- The change has been properly reviewed.
- The impact assessment is complete.
- Governance risks are acceptable.
- Related artifacts have been identified.
- Implementation is authorized.

No governance change shall be implemented before formal approval.

### 6.4 Change Implementation

Approved governance changes shall be implemented in a controlled and traceable manner.

Implementation activities shall:

- Update affected governance artifacts.
- Preserve document version history.
- Maintain traceability relationships.
- Record implementation details.
- Notify relevant stakeholders where required.

Implementation shall preserve the integrity of the governance baseline.

### 6.5 Change Verification

Implemented governance changes shall be verified before being incorporated into the approved governance baseline.

Verification shall confirm that:

- Approved changes have been correctly implemented.
- Related documents have been updated.
- Traceability has been maintained.
- Governance requirements continue to be satisfied.
- No unintended governance conflicts have been introduced.

### 6.6 Change Closure

A governance change shall be formally closed after successful verification.

Change closure shall:

- Record the final implementation status.
- Update governance records.
- Maintain historical audit information.
- Reference related governance decisions.
- Preserve complete traceability for future reviews.

## 7. Governance Change Control Rules

This section defines the operational rules governing the management of changes to governance artifacts. These rules ensure that governance changes are initiated, reviewed, approved, implemented, and recorded in a controlled and auditable manner while preserving the integrity of the governance baseline.

### 7.1 Change Request Management

Every governance change shall originate from a formally documented Change Request.

A Change Request shall include:

- A unique change request identifier.
- A clear description of the proposed change.
- The business or governance justification.
- The affected governance artifacts.
- The requestor.
- The submission date.
- Supporting documentation, where applicable.

Incomplete Change Requests shall not proceed to review.

### 7.2 Impact Assessment

Every Change Request shall undergo an impact assessment before approval.

The assessment shall evaluate:

- Impact on governance standards.
- Impact on authoritative references.
- Impact on project documentation.
- Impact on requirement traceability.
- Impact on implementation artifacts.
- Risks, dependencies, and potential conflicts.

The impact assessment shall become part of the permanent Change Record.

### 7.3 Approval Authority

Governance changes shall be approved only by the designated governance authority.

The approving authority shall verify that:

- The Change Request is complete.
- The impact assessment has been completed.
- Governance principles remain satisfied.
- Related stakeholders have been consulted, where required.
- The proposed change aligns with project objectives.

Approval shall be documented before implementation begins.

### 7.4 Version Management

Approved governance changes shall be reflected through controlled version management.

Version updates shall:

- Preserve previous document versions.
- Record revision history.
- Maintain document identity.
- Identify the effective version.
- Support historical auditability.

Version identifiers shall follow the project's document versioning standard.

### 7.5 Change Documentation

Every approved governance change shall be documented in sufficient detail to support future review and audit.

Documentation shall include:

- The approved change.
- The implementation date.
- The approving authority.
- The implementation status.
- References to related governance decisions.
- References to affected governance artifacts.

### 7.6 Governance Exceptions

Governance exceptions shall be granted only through formal approval.

Approved exceptions shall:

- Clearly define the scope of the exception.
- Identify the affected governance requirements.
- Document the business justification.
- Specify the approval authority.
- Define the duration of the exception, where applicable.

Governance exceptions shall be reviewed periodically and withdrawn when no longer required.

## 8. Governance Change Management Principles

The governance change management principles establish the architectural requirements for maintaining a controlled and resilient governance framework. These principles ensure that governance changes remain transparent, traceable, and consistent throughout the project lifecycle.

### 8.1 GOV-CHG-DATA-001 — Controlled Governance Baseline

The governance baseline shall remain under formal change control at all times.

Only approved governance changes shall modify the approved baseline.

### 8.2 GOV-CHG-DATA-002 — Complete Change Traceability

Every governance change shall maintain complete traceability from the originating Change Request through review, approval, implementation, verification, and closure.

Traceability relationships shall remain complete throughout the governance lifecycle.

### 8.3 GOV-CHG-DATA-003 — Immutable Change History

Governance change history shall be preserved as a permanent project record.

Historical records shall not be altered except through approved correction procedures that preserve the original audit trail.

### 8.4 GOV-CHG-DATA-004 — Decision Accountability

Every governance change shall be associated with a documented governance decision and an identified approving authority.

Decision records shall support accountability, transparency, and future governance reviews.

### 8.5 GOV-CHG-DATA-005 — Continuous Governance Integrity

Governance changes shall preserve the integrity, consistency, and stability of the overall governance framework.

Changes shall not introduce conflicts, ambiguity, or inconsistencies within controlled governance artifacts.

## 9. Governance Compliance Requirements

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all governance artifacts, governance activities, and governance participants within the NSS ERP project. Compliance verification ensures that governance changes are properly controlled, documented, and aligned with the approved governance framework.

### 9.1 Compliance Verification

Governance compliance shall be verified throughout the governance change lifecycle.

Verification activities shall confirm that:

- Governance changes follow the approved change control process.
- Required reviews and approvals have been completed.
- Governance documentation has been updated.
- Traceability relationships remain complete.
- Governance records are accurate and complete.

### 9.2 Non-Compliance Management

Instances of non-compliance shall be identified, documented, assessed, and resolved through the governance process.

Non-compliance management shall include:

- Identification of the non-compliance.
- Assessment of governance impact.
- Definition of corrective actions.
- Assignment of responsibility.
- Verification of corrective action completion.

Governance artifacts shall not be approved until significant non-compliance issues have been resolved unless a formally approved governance exception exists.

### 9.3 Periodic Governance Review

The governance framework shall be reviewed periodically to ensure its continued effectiveness.

Periodic reviews shall evaluate:

- Compliance with governance standards.
- Effectiveness of governance processes.
- Consistency across governance artifacts.
- Opportunities for governance improvement.
- Emerging governance risks.

Review outcomes shall be documented and retained as governance records.

## Appendix A – Governance Change Workflow

The governance change workflow defines the sequence of activities required to process governance changes from initiation through closure.

A typical governance change workflow consists of:

1. Change request submission.
2. Initial governance review.
3. Impact assessment.
4. Governance approval.
5. Controlled implementation.
6. Verification of implementation.
7. Governance baseline update.
8. Change closure.
9. Governance decision recording.

Every governance change shall follow this workflow unless an approved governance exception has been granted.
