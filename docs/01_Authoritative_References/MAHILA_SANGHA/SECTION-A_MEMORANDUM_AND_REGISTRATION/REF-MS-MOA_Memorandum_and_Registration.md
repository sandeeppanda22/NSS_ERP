# Introduction

This document serves as the authoritative reference register for the Memorandum of Association and registration particulars of the Nilachala Saraswata Mahila Sangha — a registered entity operating under the auspices of, and subordinate to, the Nilachala Saraswata Sangha (Kendra Sangha).

It preserves the original Memorandum provisions in a structured reference format without interpretation, modification, or ERP implementation guidance.

Where ambiguity or inconsistency exists, the official Bye-Law of the Nilachala Saraswata Mahila Sangha shall prevail, and where any conflict exists between this document and the NSS (Kendra Sangha) constitutional framework, the NSS framework prevails per GOV-ORG-001/002.

## Subject

Objects of the Society (Memorandum)

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-MS

---

### Constitutional Provision

**3. Objects of the Society will be —**

**a)** To function as an organisation of the female devotees of Sri Sri Paramahansa Paribrajakacharya Srimat Swamy Nigamananda Saraswati Dev (here-in-after known as "Sri Sri Thakur") under the auspices of Nilachala Saraswata Sangha (herein-after known as "Kendra Sangha") with the sole aim of materialising the ideologies of Sri Sri Thakur meant for female devotees specially those sermonised at Nilachala Kutir, Puri, the last but not the least memorable and meaningful resort of this great sage of India.

The above provision is preserved in the source under the numbered heading "3. Objects of the Society will be —" with a single lettered sub-item "a)". This is the Memorandum's own statement of objects, distinct from the fuller "1. Aims and objects" clause in the Bye-law proper (see REF-MS-1), which elaborates further.
