### Constitutional Provision

**1) Aims and objects**

**I)** To perpetuate the sacred memory of Sri Sri Thakur by properly maintaining and regularly sustaining the 'Asan' of Sri Sri Thakur at Nilachala Kutir, Swargadwar of Puri town with due regard to the rules and regulations framed by Kendra Sangha for the purpose and in compliance with the last 'will' and 'testament' of Sri Sri Thakur, so kindly communicated to the Kendra Sangha by Smt. Narayani Devi and published in Bhadra issue of Aryadarpan of 1369 Sala.

**II)** To hold regular meetings of the members of this Sangha at Nilachala Kutir, with the aim of attaining spiritual development of the members by organising discourses on the life and teachings of Sri Sri Thakur, through the guidance of Kendra Sangha.

**III)** To open training centres, seminars, and educational institutions attached to Nilachala Kutir both for teen-agers and grown-up ladies including forelorns and destitutes with the purpose of imparting both theoretical and practical training to build up ideal sevikas conducive to the fullest manifestation of Indian womenhood both as a housewife or as an Asramite.

**IV)** All functions and all activities of the Sangha including the Seva Puja at Nilachala Kutir and establishment of the training or educational centre are to be performed according to the established practices and conventions approved and direction issued by the Kendra Sangha.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "1) Aims and objects" with sub-items lettered I) through IV) (roman numerals with a closing parenthesis, as printed in the source), exactly as they appear in the official Bye-Law.
