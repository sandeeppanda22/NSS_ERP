### Statutory Provision

**2) Membership**

**I.** The minimum qualifications for being enrolled as a member of the Sangha are:—

**a)** She must have possessed a good moral character having an unshakable faith in the eternal existence of Sri Sri Thakur and must have accepted Him as the sole and supreme 'Guru' and 'Istha'.

**b)** She should be desirous of regulating her life for the purpose of Guru seva, meaning thereby Sri Sri Thakur, His devotees and the Sangha.

**c)** She should have been duly enrolled as a member of any Mahila Sangha affiliated to Nilachala Saraswata Sangha.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "2) Membership," with the introductory roman-numeral item "I." followed by lettered sub-items a) through c), exactly as they appear in the official Bye-Law.
