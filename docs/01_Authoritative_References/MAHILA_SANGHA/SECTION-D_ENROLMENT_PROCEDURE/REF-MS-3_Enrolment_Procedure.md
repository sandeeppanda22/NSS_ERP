### Statutory Provision

**3. Procedure for enrolement as member:—**

The Parichalak will enrol members of the Sangha from amongst the female devotees of Sri Sri Thakur on the basis mentioned at para (2).

The above provision is preserved in the source under the heading "3. Procedure for enrolement as member:—" as a single unlettered statement, not subdivided into further clauses. The source's own spelling "enrolement" is preserved verbatim, not corrected to "enrolment."
