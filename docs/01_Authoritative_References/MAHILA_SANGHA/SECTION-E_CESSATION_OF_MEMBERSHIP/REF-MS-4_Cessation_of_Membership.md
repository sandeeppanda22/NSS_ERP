### Statutory Provision

**4) Cessation of membership**

**a)** Membership of any member automatically ceases after her death or resignation.

**b)** On report of the Secretary of the Sangha, the Parichalak may order for cessation of membership of any member for violating the rules and regulations of the Sangha.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "4) Cessation of membership" with sub-items lettered a) and b), exactly as they appear in the official Bye-Law.
