### Constitutional Provision

**5. Constitution of the Sangha**

**i)** The Sangha will have a Governing Body consisting of—

1) President
2) Vice-President
3) Parichalak
4) Secretary
5) Joint Secretary
6) Treasurer, and 3 other members from among the members of the Sangha.

**ii)** All members of the Sangha including members of the Governing body will constitute the General Body of the Sangha.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "5. Constitution of the Sangha" with sub-items lettered i) and ii); item i) contains its own internally numbered list (1 through 6) of Governing Body office positions, exactly as they appear in the official Bye-Law.
