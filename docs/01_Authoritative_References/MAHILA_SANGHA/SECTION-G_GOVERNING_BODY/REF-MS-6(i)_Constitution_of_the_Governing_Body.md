### Statutory Provision

**6. Governing Body**

**i) Constitution:—**

**a)** Over all management of the Sangha will be entrusted to a Governing Body of 9 members including Smt. Surabala Devi and Smt. Narayani Devi the two original sevikas who will be there as the Founder President and the Permanent Vice-President respectively till they prefer to serve in these capacities.

**b)** In this capacity as the Secretary and Parichalak of Nilachala Saraswata Sangha, Sri D. C. Mohanty will be a life member of this Sangha and will function as its Parichalaka.

**c)** The remaining six members of the Governing Body will be nominated by the Parichalaka with the knowledge of the President.

**d)** The Governing Body thus formed will hold office for a term of two years from the date they assume office after the registration of this Sangha, provided that they shall continue as such even after the prescribed period of two years till a new Governing Body is constituted and takes over charge.

**e)** After expiry of the term of the Governing Body, the Parichalaka will take the consensus of opinion of the members of the General Body and announce the list of members with the consent of the President. He will also announce the names of the office-bearers, mentioned in Para 5 from amongst the members of the Governing Body in consultation with the President.

**f)** On the resignation or non-existence of the present President or the present Vice-President, the Governing Body in consultation with Parichalaka will elect its President or Vice-President as the case may be in a meeting of the General Body, convened by the Secretary for this purpose.

**g)** If any vacancy arises out of the non-existence, or resignation of any member of the Governing Body the same will be filled up by the Parichalak from amongst the members of the Sangha for the unexpired portion of the term.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "6. Governing Body" → "i) Constitution:—" with sub-items lettered a) through g), exactly as they appear in the official Bye-Law. Minor OCR/typing artifacts in the source ("even after" printed as "ever after" in one transcription) have been corrected as an obvious typographical error, not a substantive change.
