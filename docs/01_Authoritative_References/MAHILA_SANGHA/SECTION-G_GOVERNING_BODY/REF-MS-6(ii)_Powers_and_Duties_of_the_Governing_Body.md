### Statutory Provision

**ii) Powers and duties of the Governing Body**

**a)** The Governing Body shall have the powers to frame rules and regulations for the proper management of the Sangha.

They shall frame rules and regulations from time to time for regulating the general conduct of the members of the Sangha so that the desires of the Sri Sri Thakur might have conveniently fulfilled both in spirit and action.

**c)** The Governing Body shall also frame rules for running institutions like training centres, seminars and other educational institutions including the existing Sanskrit Pathasala, attached to Nilachala Kutir.

**d)** The Governing Body shall maintain proper accounts of all the receipts including different kinds of Pranamis, donations and grants for different purposes and disbursement of Sangha funds on different accounts.

**e)** They will prepare their own annual budget estimate of the income and expenditure of the Sangha.

**f)** They will be competent to hold and acquire properties as well as dispose of the same in furtherance of the aims and objects of the Sangha.

**g)** They can offer Pranami to Saraswata Mutha, and Gurudham, as well as take decisions to contribute towards any scheme for materialisation of the ideologies of Sri Sri Thakur.

**h)** They will arrange to keep a specific hour fixed in the daily programme of Nilachala Kutir for the Darsan of Sri Sri Thakur, which will be open to all categories of His disciples and devotees without any discrimination. Specially the disciples and those who owe allegiance to the Saraswata Matha of Kokilamukh at Assam will be given due respect and will be received with full cordiality when any of them arrive for such 'Darsan.'

**i)** They will take necessary steps for proper management of the landed property situated at village Taradapada, P. S. Nimapara, in the district of Puri, recorded in the name of the President Smt. Surabala Devi as Marfatdar, and the net produce thereof or sale proceeds as the case may be shall be spent in strict conformity with the instructions issued from time to time by Smt. Surabala Devi, the President and Smt. Narayani Devi, the Vice-President, either jointly or severally. In the absence of any such instructions or in the circumstances when no such instructions can possibly be issued by either of the two Adi Sevikas named above, the Parichalaka will be competent to spend the same in any manner he thinks proper, but within the scope of this bye-law.

**J)** All possible steps should be taken for proper preservation of all articles attached with the sacred memory of Sri Sri Thakur handed over to the Nilachala Saraswata Sangha by the President vide the list furnished with her letter to the said Sangha and also published in Arya Darpan of Bhadra issue of 1369 Sal.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "ii) Powers and duties of the Governing Body" with sub-items lettered a), then an unlabeled paragraph, then c) through i), and a final item printed as "J)" (capital J, as in the source) rather than lowercase "j)". **This is confirmed as the actual structure of the source: there is no labeled sub-item "b)" — the source's own printed text moves directly from "a)" to an unlabeled paragraph and then to "c)".** This gap and the capitalisation of the final "J)" have been preserved exactly as printed, not corrected or relettered, consistent with the project's source-fidelity rule (see the equivalent treatment applied to NSS's Advisory Board clause, REF-003-D).
