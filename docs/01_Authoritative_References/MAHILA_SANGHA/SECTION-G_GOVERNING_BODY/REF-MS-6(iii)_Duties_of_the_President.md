### Statutory Provision

**iii) Duties of the President**

**I)** In addition to the different functions of the President mentioned in different paragraphs of the article, her other function shall be as under —

**a)** She shall preside over all the meetings of the Sangha and Governing body.

**b)** All important policy decision pertaining to the implementation of the pious desires of Sri Sri Thakur must have her prior approval.

**c)** She can delegate her powers to any member of the Governing Body for proper discharge of the Presidential duties on her behalf.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "iii) Duties of the President" with an introductory roman-numeral item "I)" followed by lettered sub-items a) through c), exactly as they appear in the official Bye-Law.
