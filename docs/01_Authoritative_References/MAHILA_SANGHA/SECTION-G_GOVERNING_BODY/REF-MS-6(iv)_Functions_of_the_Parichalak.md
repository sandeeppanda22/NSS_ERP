### Constitutional Provision

**iv) Functions of the Parichalak**

**I)** In addition to the functions of the Parichalak as mentioned in different paragraphs of the articles, his functions vis-à-vis this Sangha will be —

**a)** to act as the guardian of the inmates of Nilachala Kutir and shall remain in over all charge of entire activities of the Sangha.

**b)** to look to the effective preservation and utilisation of all the properties belonging to the Sangha.

**c)** to appoint the Sevaks and Sevikas for performing day-to-day Seva Puja of Sri Sri Thakur.

**d)** to impart Satsikshya (spiritual teachings) both theoretical and practical to the member of the Sangha.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "iv) Functions of the Parichalak" with an introductory roman-numeral item "I)" followed by lettered sub-items a) through d), exactly as they appear in the official Bye-Law. The source's spelling "apoint" in item c) has been silently corrected to "appoint" as an obvious typographical error, not a substantive change.
