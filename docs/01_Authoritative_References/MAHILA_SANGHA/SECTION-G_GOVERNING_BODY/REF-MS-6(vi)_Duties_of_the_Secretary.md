### Statutory Provision

**vi) Duties of the Secretary**

**1)** She will be in charge of the office and will maintain all records and accounts of the Sangha.

The above provision is preserved in the source under the heading "vi) Duties of the Secretary" with a single numbered item "1)", not subdivided into further clauses.
