# Introduction

This document serves as the authoritative reference register for sub-clause (vii) of Clause 6 ("Governing Body") of the Bye-Law of Nilachala Saraswata Mahila Sangha. There is no directly equivalent office in the NSS Bye-Law (NSS has an "Assistant Secretary," see REF-003-C(i)(6)).

### Constitutional Provision

**vii) Duties of Joint Secretary** — She will assist the Secretary in all her works specified in para 6 (vi).

The above provision is preserved in the source under the heading "vii) Duties of Joint Secretary" as a single unlettered statement, not subdivided into further clauses.
