### Constitutional Provision

**viii) Duties of Treasurer** — She will maintain proper accounts of all the receipts and disbursements of the Sangha.

The above provision is preserved in the source under the heading "viii) Duties of Treasurer" as a single unlettered statement, not subdivided into further clauses.
