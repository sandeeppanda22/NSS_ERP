### Statutory Provision

**7. FUNDS**

**i) Funds of the Sangha shall comprise of all the following—**

**a)** Pranamis of various categories.

**b)** Voluntary donations received from the devotees and patronisers.

**c)** Donations of various kinds for specific purpose.

**d)** Grants from Government, Semi-Government and Non-Official Bodies including Kendra Sangha.

**e)** Miscellaneous contributions.

**f)** Earnings from landed properties and other sources, if any.

**g)** Landed properties held in the name of the President.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "7. FUNDS" → "i) Funds of the Sangha shall comprise of all the following—" with sub-items lettered a) through g), exactly as they appear in the official Bye-Law.
