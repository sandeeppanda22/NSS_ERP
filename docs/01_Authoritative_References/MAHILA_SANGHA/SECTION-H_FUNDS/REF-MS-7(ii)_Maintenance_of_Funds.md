### Constitutional Provision

**ii) Maintenance of funds**

**1)** Funds so collected shall be kept in a Postal savings Bank or State Bank of India or in both, to be operated jointly by the Treasurer and the Secretary of the Sangha.

The above provision is preserved in the source under the heading "ii) Maintenance of funds" with a single numbered item "1)", not subdivided into further clauses. The source's phrase "State Bank or of India" is preserved verbatim as printed; it is presumed to mean "State Bank of India," but no correction has been applied since this constitutes a substantive wording choice, not an obvious transcription artifact like a misspelling.
