### Constitutional Provision

**iii) Utilisation of funds**

**a)** Funds so realised may be spent by the Governing Body for the following purposes in consultation with the Parichalak of Nilachala Saraswata Sangha.

**b)** Annual celebration of the Birth Day Ceremony of Shri Shri Thakura at Nilachala Kutir.

**c)** Meeting day-to-day expenditure of Nilachala Kutir, Puri.

**d)** Running the training centres, educational institutions including the existing Sanskrit Pathasala and seminar etc.

**e)** Publication of books and meeting the expenditure on Sri Sri Thakur's life, teachings and philosophy.

**f)** Maintaining the cleanliness and peace and tranquility befitting the sublime, dignified and living memory of Shri Shri Thakur and serenity of the compound.

**g)** Nevertheless the annual repair works both minor and major, necessary additions and alterations to the buildings of the Kutir, payment of land revenue, municipal tax and other charges along with the proper maintenance of land records in the name of Shri Shri Thakur shall always be done by Nilachala Saraswata Sangha as they have been doing the same since long.

**h)** Contributions to different activities sponsored by Nilachala Saraswata Sangha to materialise the ideologies of Shri Shri Thakur, when so required.

**i)** All items of expenditure should be supported by a relevant resolution of the Governing Body.

**j)** Provided that any donation received for any specific purpose shall be spent and utilised for that purpose alone.

**k)** For expenses of President and Vice-President.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "iii) Utilisation of funds" with sub-items lettered a) through k), exactly as they appear in the official Bye-Law. The source's spelling "types" printed as "times" in item i) ("All times of expenditure") has been corrected to "items" as an obvious transcription error, not a substantive change.
