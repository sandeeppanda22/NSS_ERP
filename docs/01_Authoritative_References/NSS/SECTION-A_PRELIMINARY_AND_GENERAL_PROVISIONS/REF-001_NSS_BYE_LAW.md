# Introduction

This document serves as the statutory reference register for the Bye-Law of the Nilachala Saraswata Sangha.

It preserves the statutory provisions in a structured reference format and shall not be interpreted as modifying, replacing, or superseding the official Memorandum of Association and Bye-Law of the Nilachala Saraswata Sangha.

The section order below follows the confirmed sequence of the official source exactly: Name of the Society, Location of the Registered Office, Special Features : Preamble, a short introductory "Objects of the Society" paragraph, the Memorandum of Association (numbered "6." in the source, with no separate heading text), and finally the fuller restatement of "Objects of the Society" containing the "Bye-law Of Nilachala Saraswata Sangha" sub-heading and the complete numbered list of aims and objects (1 through 20). This sequence — including the repeated "3. Objects of the Society" heading and the numbering jump from 3 directly to 6 with no intervening 4 or 5 — has been confirmed against the source and is not a repository error.

# REF-001-001 — Special Features : Preamble

## Subject

Special Features : Preamble

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-001

---

### Statutory Provision

**Special Features : PREAMBLE** — Paramahansa Paribrajakacharya Srimat Swami Nigamananda Saraswati Deb (hereinafter mentioned as 'Shri Shri Thakur') felt a divine urge and himself established this 'Nilachala Saraswata Sangha' at Puri on the 24th August 1934 on his 'Holy Birthday' (Srabana Purnima) and blessed this institution by becoming its Founder President. Since then as the trustworthy retainer, faithful propounder and honest propagator of the philosophy and outlook underlying the ideologies of Shri Shri Thakur, the Kendra Sangha has been functioning and shall function as such in Orissa and beyond its limits if circumstances so necessitate, as the supreme organisation and the nerve-centre of all its branches (Sakha Sanghas) along with all attached family organisations (Paribarik Sanghas) who have accepted with all its implications the cardinal principles and special features of the 'Kendra Sangha' by recognising 'GURU' and 'ISTA' as one and the same supreme being and entertaining an unshakable faith in the existence of Shri Shri Gurudev through His Name (Jayaguru Sidha Mantra), Rupa (Internal Existence), Leela (discourse on his life and teachings amongst the devotees) and Bigraha (worship of his image in the Asana Mandir).

The above provision is preserved in the source under the heading "Special Features : PREAMBLE" as a single continuous narrative paragraph, not subdivided into numbered or lettered clauses. This heading and content is positioned between "2. Location of the Registered Office" and "3. Objects of the Society" in the source — not at the top of Section A as in an earlier version of this document. Minor OCR artifacts from the source scan (e.g. "beyod" and "A sana") have been silently corrected to "beyond" and "Asana" as obvious transcription errors, not substantive changes.

Objects of the Society (introductory paragraph)

---

### Source Reference

### Statutory Provision

**3. Objects of the Society :**

The aims and objects of the Sangha shall be:

To materialise the ideologies of Shri Shri Thakur i.e. 'Sanatana Dharma Prachar', 'Satsikshya Bistar', and 'Siva jnane jeeva Seva' through 'Adarsha Grihasth Jeevan Gathan', 'Sangha Saktira Pratistha' and 'Bhaba Binimay' amongst the household devotees of Shri Shri Thakur in the manner prescribed by him personally at Nilachala Kutir, Puri, the last but not the least memorable and meaningful resort of this great sage of India.

The above provision is preserved in the source under the heading "3. Objects of the Society :" as a short introductory paragraph. This is distinct from, and precedes, the fuller restatement of "Objects of the Society" (also numbered "3.") which follows the Memorandum of Association (REF-001-005) and contains the "Bye-law Of Nilachala Saraswata Sangha" sub-heading and the complete numbered list of 20 objects (see REF-001-004-B).

Objects of the Society (full restatement, numbered list)

### Statutory Provision

**3. OBJECTS OF THE SOCIETY:**

**Bye-law Of Nilachala Saraswata Sangha**

**Aims and objects of the Association (Sangha)**

**Further Rules shall be :**

**1.** To perpetuate the sacred memory of Shri Shri Thakur by suitably maintaining and regularly sustaining the 'Asana' and 'Bigraha' of Shri Shri Thakur at Nilachala Kutir, Swargadwar, Puri.

**2.** To conduct the day-to-day seva pujas of Shri Shri Thakur at Nilachala Kutir, Puri which will include all 'Nitya' and 'Naimittika' pujas following the established practices and conventions through some righteous house-hold devotee preferably a Brahmin devotee or in case of non-availability of such a Brahmin or non-Brahmin devotee to run it, through a righteous Brahmin having full faith in Shri Shri Thakur.

**3.** To take all possible measures for the proper preservation of all articles of Nilachala Kutir which were used by Shri Shri Thakur personally and are associated with the sacred memory of Shri Shri Thakur.

**4.** To take necessary steps for the proper maintenance and necessary improvements of the Nilachala Kutir including all other movable and immovable properties and arrange payment of land revenue and other taxes etc. for these from the Sangha funds and special donations, if any received for this purpose from the devotees, as the Sangha has been doing since the date from which management of Nilachala Kutir was so kindly entrusted to it and published as a news item in 'Aryadarpana' of Bhadraba, 1369 Sal (1962 AD) by Smt. Narayani Devi in the year 1962.

**5.** To suitably maintain and regularly sustain, the 'Asan' of Shri Shri Thakur in the Nigamananda Smruti Mandir, Swargadwar, Puri as the 'Asan' of the Kendra Sangha by conducting day-to-day seva pujas of all kinds (Nitya, Naimittika and Kamya) and to make necessary arrangements for the proper upkeep and maintenance of the articles associated with his memory which are existing at present in the Nigamananda Smruti Mandir. In this connection, the hand-rikshaw which was used by Shri Shri Thakur at Puri, deserves special attention. Due attention shall also be paid for preservation of other articles which will be collected and preserved in the Smruti Mandir by the Kendra Sangha in future. The Kendra Sangha shall also take necessary measures for the proper maintenance and necessary improvements of the Smruti Mandir including payment of land revenue and all other taxes etc. in time from the funds of the Kendra Sangha and donations which will be received for this purpose.

**6.** Suitably maintain and regularly sustain the Bhakta Sammilani 'Asan' and 'Sikhya Kendra Asan' at Biratung, PS. Gop, Dist. Puri by conducting the day-to-day seva puja of all kinds Nitya, Naimittika, and Kamya and to run the 'Sikshya Kendra' (Training Centre) there for imparting training both theoretical and practical for the intellectual and spiritual attainments of different categories of pupils (Bhaktas, Sevakas and students) on the lines enunciated by Shri Shri Thakur.

**7.** To recognise the sanctity of Saraswat Math and Gurudham at Kutabpur not only by offering Pranamis on specified occasions but also including the spirit of services to be rendered to these institutions with a view to materialise the ideologies of Shri Shri Thakur.

**8.** To take necessary steps for the proper upkeep and maintenance of the articles used by Shri Shri Thakur and associated with his memory which are still preserved at Biratung, P.S. Gop, Dist. Puri.

**9.** To take necessary measures for providing accommodation both for the 'trainees', 'teachers', Bhaktas and 'Sevaks' for the smooth running of the Training Centres. Suitable steps should also be taken for the proper maintenance and necessary improvement of the 'Sikshya Kendra' and 'Asana Mandir' at Biratung including payment of land revenue and other taxes etc. in time.

**10.** To open training-centres for the culture of different branches of fine arts like painting, music, dancing, singing, asana sajja (decoration), stitching etc. at suitable places with the avowed aim of utilising this training and knowledge for the seva pujas and ceremonial functions of Shri Shri Thakur. The knowledge thus obtained shall not be used for any other public purpose.

**11.** To sponsor the establishment of Nilachala Saraswata Mahila Sangha under the auspices of the Kendra Sangha at Nilachala Kutir, Puri for the spiritual intellectual development of the female devotees of Shri Shri Thakur in compliance with the last 'wish and Testament' of Shri Shri Thakur so kindly communicated to the Kendra Sangha and published in Aryadarpana of Bhadra Issue, 1369 Sal by Smt. Narayani Devi.

**12.** To accord permission for the establishment of Sakha Sanghas (Branches of Nilachala Saraswata Sangha) and 'Asana Mandir' at different places for propagation, discussion and implementation of Shri Shri Thakur's ideas, ideologies and teaching. The Office-bearers of these Sakha Sanghas will function as the custodians of the said 'Asana Mandir' and 'Sakha Sangha' on behalf of the Kendra Sangha. To accord affiliation to such 'Asana Mandir' after due enquiry in the prescribed manner of such prayers by offering token memory (Smruti Chihna) of Shri Shri Thakur and issuing a letter of authority to establish the Asana Mandir and Sakha Sanghas on performance of due ceremonies. But no Sakha Sangha or Asana Mandir will qualify to be affiliated unless at least three of the recognised devotees of Shri Shri Thakur are there in the habit of offering daily Seva and Pujas to Shri Shri Thakur by establishing Paribarik Asanas at their respective residences. To ensure that all sorts of Seva and Pujas including Sangha Adhibesans should be conducted through the introduction of 'Pali' system both at the Kendra Sangha and the 'Sakha' level. To organise 'Mahila Sanghas' in different 'Sakha Sanghas' for the female devotees which will function as a part of the concerned Sakha Sangha and the Kendra Sangha.

**13.** To conduct and guide the functioning of different 'Zilla Sanghas' and other occasional functions and ceremonies organised by different Sakha Sanghas from time to time.

**14.** To make arrangements for the annual Session of the 'Utkal Pradeshik Bhakta Sammilani' and for holding the sessions of 'Sarbabhoum Bhakta Sammilani' when such a decision is taken by the Governing body of the Kendra Sangha.

**15.** To celebrate the sacred 'Janmostsava' (Birthday ceremony) of Shri Shri Thakur at Nilachala Kutir, Puri annually on each fullmoon day (Purnima) of the Oriya month Shrabana (August), popularly known as 'Gahma Purnima', as has been the usual practice so long and other special functions and ceremonies with due rituals and sanctity. The anniversary of the Kendra Sangha shall also be celebrated on the same 'Janmotsava Day'. Each and every affiliated Sakha Sangha including the Mahila Sanghas shall submit an Annual Report of their activities and accounts to the Governing Body on or before the 'Janmostsav Day' on receipt of which the Governing Body will arrange for the renewal of recognition of the respective Sakha Sanghas and Mahila Sanghas. The Secretary of the Governing Body or anybody on his behalf shall also arrange to place the Annual Report of the Kendra Sangha on its activities and accounts for discussion and approval before the General Body at the time of its annual session for information.

**16.** To prepare and publish books and journals etc. dealing with the concepts and philosophy of Shri Shri Thakur (a) translation of his own writings and other literatures on his life and philosophy to Oriya, (b) by producing other corroborating literatures and (c) by encouraging research in these matters.

**17.** To make necessary arrangements for providing all possible facilities to the Parichalak of the Sangha with a view to assist him in his pursuit of research and production of literatures on Shri Shri Thakur's teachings and philosophy which may include besides other facilities, maintenance of a library, provision of accommodation and services of suitable assistants and attendants for the purpose. Special allotment of funds should also be made for meeting the personal expenditure of the Parichalak in order to maintain his efficiency for the work including discretionary grant to be placed at his disposal, which will not be subject to any audit.

**18.** To perpetuate the sacred memory of Shri Shri Thakur's first visit to Puri as Paribrajaka and his sojourn at Room Nos. 19 and 20 in Bagala Dharmasala, by opening a suitable Reading Room therein for which the authorities of the Lodging House Fund Committee, Puri have been pleased to accord necessary permission on rental basis. In this connection, it may be further noted that the Sarbabhoum Bhakta Sammilani being convened by Shri Shri Thakur himself on behalf of the Nilachal Saraswata Sangha was also held in the said Dharmasala at his instance for the first time in Orissa in the year 1936. To frame rules and make necessary provisions in the Annual Budget of the Kendra Sangha for the proper maintenance of the Reading Room in Bagala Dharmasala, which should contain literatures on Shri Shri Thakur's life, teachings and philosophy, both in books and journals along with other corroborating scriptures of Sanatan Dharma.

**19.** To undertake the construction of Bhakta Nivas, Mahila Nivas, Chhatrabas, Gosala, Vidyapitha, Atithi Bhavan, Parichalak Nivas, Chikitsa Bhavan etc. at suitable places if and when funds so permit.

**20.** To undertake any other activity, which will be required for fulfillment of the purpose of Shri Shri Thakur in establishing the Nilachala Saraswata Sangha.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the second appearance of the heading "3. OBJECTS OF THE SOCIETY:" — following the Memorandum of Association (REF-001-005) — introduced by the "Bye-law Of Nilachala Saraswata Sangha / Aims and objects of the Association (Sangha) / Further Rules shall be :" sub-heading block, then the flat numbered list (1 through 20), not subdivided into lettered sub-clauses except where the original text itself uses internal lettering (see item 16, which contains internal sub-points (a), (b), (c)).
