# REF-001-001 — Preamble

## Subject

Preamble

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-001

---

### Constitutional Provision

#### Clause 1

Paramahansa Paribrajakacharya Srimat Swami Nigamananda Saraswati Deb (hereinafter referred to as **Shri Shri Thakur**) established the Nilachala Saraswata Sangha at Puri on **24 August 1934 (Shrabana Purnima)** and became its Founder President.

#### Clause 2

The Kendra Sangha functions as the faithful custodian, trustworthy propagator, and promoter of the philosophy, ideals, and teachings of Shri Shri Thakur.

#### Clause 3

The Kendra Sangha serves as the supreme organisation and central institution of all Sakha Sanghas and affiliated family organisations established under its authority.

#### Clause 4

All affiliated organisations recognise Guru and Ista as one Supreme Being and maintain unwavering faith in Shri Shri Gurudev through:

- Jayaguru Siddha Mantra (Name)
- Rupa
- Leela
- Bigraha

# REF-001-004 — Objects of the Society

## Subject

Objects of the Society

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-001

---

### Constitutional Provision

#### Clause 1

To perpetuate the sacred memory of Shri Shri Thakur by suitably maintaining and regularly sustaining the Asana and Bigraha of Shri Shri Thakur at Nilachala Kutir, Swargadwar, Puri.

#### Clause 2

To conduct the day-to-day Seva and Pujas of Shri Shri Thakur at Nilachala Kutir, including all Nitya and Naimittika Pujas, in accordance with the established traditions and conventions through a righteous household devotee as prescribed in the Constitution.

#### Clause 3

To take all possible measures for the proper preservation of all articles personally used by Shri Shri Thakur and associated with His sacred memory at Nilachala Kutir.

#### Clause 4

To maintain, preserve and improve Nilachala Kutir and all its movable and immovable properties, including payment of land revenue, taxes and other statutory obligations from Sangha funds and designated donations.

#### Clause 5

To suitably maintain and regularly sustain the Asana of Shri Shri Thakur at the Nigamananda Smruti Mandir through regular Seva and Pujas, preserve articles associated with His memory, and maintain the Smruti Mandir and its properties.

#### Clause 6

To suitably maintain and regularly sustain the Bhakta Sammilani Asana and Sikshya Kendra Asana at Biratung, conduct all prescribed Seva and Pujas, and operate the Sikshya Kendra for the intellectual and spiritual training of devotees, Sevakas and students.

#### Clause 7

To recognise the sanctity of Saraswat Math and Gurudham at Kutabpur through offerings and services with the objective of fulfilling the ideologies of Shri Shri Thakur.

#### Clause 8

To preserve and maintain all articles associated with Shri Shri Thakur that are presently preserved at Biratung.

#### Clause 9

To provide accommodation and facilities for trainees, teachers, Bhaktas and Sevakas and ensure the proper maintenance and development of the Sikshya Kendra and Asana Mandir at Biratung.

#### Clause 10

To establish training centres for fine arts including painting, music, dancing, singing, Asana decoration, stitching and related disciplines for use exclusively in the Seva, Pujas and ceremonial functions of Shri Shri Thakur.

#### Clause 11

To establish and support the Nilachala Saraswata Mahila Sangha under the auspices of the Kendra Sangha for the spiritual and intellectual development of women devotees in accordance with the wishes of Shri Shri Thakur.

#### Clause 12

To accord permission for the establishment of Sakha Sanghas and Asana Mandirs for the propagation, discussion and implementation of the teachings of Shri Shri Thakur; to recognise and affiliate such institutions in the prescribed manner; to regulate their functioning; and to encourage the establishment of Mahila Sanghas as integral parts of affiliated Sakha Sanghas.

#### Clause 13

To conduct, guide and supervise the functioning of Zilla Sanghas and other ceremonial functions organised by affiliated Sakha Sanghas.

#### Clause 14

To organise the annual sessions of the Utkal Pradeshik Bhakta Sammilani and, whenever approved by the Governing Body, the sessions of the Sarbabhoum Bhakta Sammilani.

#### Clause 15

To celebrate the annual Janmotsava of Shri Shri Thakur and the anniversary of the Kendra Sangha; to receive annual reports and accounts from affiliated Sanghas; to renew their recognition; and to place the annual report and accounts of the Kendra Sangha before the General Body.

#### Clause 16

To prepare, publish, translate and promote books, journals and research relating to the life, teachings and philosophy of Shri Shri Thakur.

#### Clause 17

To provide the Parichalak with facilities necessary for research and publication relating to the teachings and philosophy of Shri Shri Thakur, including library facilities, accommodation, assistants, financial support and discretionary grants as provided under the Constitution.

#### Clause 18

To perpetuate the sacred memory of Shri Shri Thakur's first visit to Puri by maintaining a Reading Room at Bagala Dharmasala and preserving literature relating to His life, teachings and philosophy.

#### Clause 19

To frame rules and provide financial support for the maintenance and operation of the Reading Room at Bagala Dharmasala and ensure the availability of literature on Shri Shri Thakur and Sanatana Dharma.

#### Clause 20

To establish institutions including Bhakta Nivas, Mahila Nivas, Chhatrabas, Gosala, Vidyapitha, Atithi Bhavan, Parichalak Nivas, Chikitsa Bhavan and any other institutions or activities necessary to fulfil the purpose for which Shri Shri Thakur established the Nilachala Saraswata Sangha.
