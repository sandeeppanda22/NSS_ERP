# REF-002-001 — Membership Categories

Membership Categories

### Constitutional Provision

**(B) Memberships** — The Kendra Sangha shall have three categories of Members:- (i) Probationary members (ii) Regular members and (iii) Associate members.

# REF-002-002 — Probationary Members

## Subject

Probationary Members

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-002

---

### Constitutional Provision

**(a) Probationary members:** The minimum qualification for being enrolled as a 'probationary member' of the Kendra Sangha are:

(i) One must have possessed a good moral character and must have accepted Shri Shri Thakur as the sole and supreme 'Guru' and 'Ista'.

(ii) Must be observing the 'Niyam Panchak' as prescribed by the Governing Body.

(iii) Should abide by the rules and regulations as detailed in 'Sangha Neeti' promulgated by the Kendra Sangha from time to time.

(iv) Should have resolved to dedicate himself as a Sevak of Shri Shri Thakur, His devotees and the Sangha.

(v) Should be leading a household life and should have the aim of developing it into 'Adarsha Grihastha Jeevan' as enunciated by Shri Shri Thakur.

On being enrolled as probationary member on the basis of the above qualifications, he or she shall be issued an 'Anumati Patra' signed and issued by the Kendra Sangha on performance of due ceremonies.

On being a member, his or her conduct must strictly confine to the directions given in the 'Anumati Patra' for a 'Sangha Sevi' and the member should recognise the sanctity of Saraswata Matha, Guru Dham and owe allegiance to 'Nilachala Kutir' and 'Smruti Mandir' at Puri and 'Sikshya Kendra' at Biratung, Dist: Puri. A probationary member shall have powers to vote in all matters relating to the concerned Sakha Sangha and in the General Body meeting of the Kendra Sangha, but he is not eligible to be the President or Secretary of the Sakha Sangha.

Under special circumstances, such members can also function as President or Secretary of the Sakha Sangha with approval of the Parichalak, as special cases, which should not be cited as precedent cases.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "(a) Probationary members" with the five qualification criteria numbered (i) through (v), followed by three unlettered narrative paragraphs, not further subdivided in the official Bye-Law.

# REF-002-003 — Regular Members

## Subject

Regular Members

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-002

---

### Constitutional Provision

**(b) Regular members:**

(i) In order to be a regular member of the Kendra Sangha one must have continued as a probationary member of any Sakha Sangha including Mahila Sanghas holding valid 'Anumati Patra' at least for a period of one year.

(ii) On recommendation of the Sakha, one must have undergone training for atleast one year under the guidance of the Kendra Sangha.

(iii) On completion of the training period, he or she can be enrolled as a full-fledged member of the Kendra Sangha and shall be issued an Identity Card (Parichaya Patra) by the Kendra Sangha.

(iv) On being enrolled as a member of the Kendra Sangha, he or she shall have to culture an aptitude for actively participating in all the activities undertaken by the Kendra Sangha in addition to his/her responsibilities already voluntarily shouldered by him/her as seva in the Sakha Sangha.

(v) The Parichalak can suo motto enrol directly any devotee as a regular member of the Kendra Sangha and subsequently direct him to be member of any Sakha Sangha.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "(b) Regular members" with sub-items numbered (i) through (v), not further subdivided in the official Bye-Law.

# REF-002-004 — Associate Members

## Subject

Associate Members

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-002

---

### Constitutional Provision

**(c) Associate Members** — The Parichalak, suo motto, or on the recommendation of any Sakha Sangha can enrol any person as Associate member taking into consideration his or her active participation and sympathetic attitude towards implementation of any scheme undertaken by Kendra Sangha.

Such members will be entitled to attend all the functions of the Sakha Sanghas and those of the Kendra Sangha but will not be entitled to be elected as, or to elect any representative to any elected posts both of the Sakha and the Kendra Sangha.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "(c) Associate Members" as two unlettered narrative paragraphs, not subdivided into further clauses in the official Bye-Law.

# REF-002-005 — Cessation of Membership

## Subject

Cessation of Membership

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-002

---

### Constitutional Provision

**(d) Cessation of Membership:**

(i) The membership of any member automatically ceases after death, non-renewal of the Annual Parichaya Patra (Identity Card) or Anumati Patra (Admit Card) as the case may be or absence in three consecutive weekly Sangha Pujas without permission of the President of the concerned Sakha Sangha.

(ii) Membership of the Kendra Sangha automatically ceases with the cessation of membership in any Sakha Sangha if not otherwise directed by the Parichalak to continue as such.

(iii) On receipt of a report either from the President of the Sakha Sangha or from any member of the Governing Body of the Kendra Sangha, membership of any member will cease on the following grounds:

(a) for the misdemeanours of the members.

(b) for indulging in activities contrary to the ideologies of Shri Shri Thakur.

(c) for violating the Rules and Regulations of the Sakha or the Kendra Sangha.

(d) for lack of faith in the ideologies of Shri Shri Thakur subsequently; and

(e) for reasons of moral turpitude.

Person who ceases to be a member of the Kendra Sangha shall not be allowed to take part in any activity of the Kendra Sangha.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "(d) Cessation of Membership" with sub-items numbered (i) through (iii), sub-item (iii) further lettered (a) through (e), followed by a closing unlettered narrative statement, exactly as they appear in the official Bye-Law.
