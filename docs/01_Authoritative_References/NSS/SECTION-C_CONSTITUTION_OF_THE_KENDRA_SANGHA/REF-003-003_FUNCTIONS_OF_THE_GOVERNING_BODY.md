### Constitutional Provision

#### Clause 1

The overall management of all the affairs of the Kendra Sangha shall be entrusted to the Governing Body which will take decisions on policy matters according to the lines indicated by Shri Shri Thakur and remain as the custodian of all the properties, movable and immovable, pertaining to Nilachala Kutir and Smruti Mandir at Puri and the Sikshya Kendra at Biratung, P.S-Gop, Dist-Puri which will be recorded in the name of Shri Shri Thakur under the maarfatdarship of Kendra Sangha in all revenue municipal and other concerned records.

---

#### Clause 2

The Governing Body shall have powers to frame rules and regulations for the proper management of Seva and Pujas of all types — Nitya, Naimittika and Kamya in the Nilachala Kutir, Smruti Mandir at Swargadwar, Puri and the Sikshya Kendra at Biratung, P.S. Gop, Dist. Puri along with all the branch institutions including the Mahila Sanghas run by or affiliated to the Kendra Sangha.

---

#### Clause 3

It shall also frame rules and regulations for the proper functioning of the Sakha Sanghas including Mahila Sanghas, Sakha Asana Mandirs and Paribarik Asana Mandir.

---

#### Clause 4

It shall frame rules and regulations for the conduct and daily duties of all types of Sevaks and the general conduct of the members of the Kendra and Sakha Sanghas by framing the 'Sangha Neeti' on the lines indicated by Shri Shri Thakur.

---

#### Clause 5

It shall give affiliation to different 'Sakha Sanghas' and 'Sakha Asanas' according to the lines indicated by Shri Shri Thakur for the formation of a Sakha Sangha and establishment of a Sakha Asana, including Paribarik Asana.

---

#### Clause 6

It shall also frame rules for the proper management and functioning of different categories of training centres as mentioned in paragraph -6 and 8.

---

#### Clause 7

It shall frame rules for the observance of Pancha Yagnas not only by the individual members (Sangha Sevis) and branch organisations, but also for the Kendra Sangha itself at suitable places.

---

#### Clause 8

It shall appoint suitable sevaks for Nilachala Kutir and Smruti Mandir at Puri and Sikshya Kendra at Biratung, P.S. Gop, Dist. Puri, if necessary on remuneration basis.

---

#### Clause 9

It may appoint a paid Manager and other staff if required, for the proper maintenance of all accounts of the Kendra Sangha and for the proper management of all the properties of the Kendra Sangha under the guidance of the Governing Body.

---

#### Clause 10

It shall make suitable arrangements for proper celebration of 'Janmotsava' (Holy Birthday Ceremony) of Shri Shri Thakur on each Srabana Purnima day, popularly known as 'Gahma Purnima' or 'Jhulan Purnima' day and other ceremonial functions as decided by the Kendra Sangha from time to time.

---

#### Clause 11

It shall be competent for making arrangements for initiation (Name Grahana) of those desirable devotees who are desirous of being initiated with 'Jayaguru' Sidhamantra in the manner prescribed by the Kendra Sangha and also as laid down in 'Meru Tantra' through the process of Ghata Sthapan.

---

#### Clause 12

It shall also frame rules for the proper celebration of the annual session of the 'Utkal Pradeshik Bhakta Sammilani' and shall look to its proper management on the lines sermonised by Shri Shri Thakur.

---

#### Clause 13

It shall have to pass an annual budget for the Kendra Sangha taking into consideration the anticipated income and expenditure for the year concerned. But before the budget for the year is finally prepared for its consideration, the draft budget or any other draft resolution entailing financial implication should be referred to the Advisory Board for their considered views to be furnished to the Governing Body within a reasonable period of time. The draft budget or the resolution as the case may be, would be placed before the Governing Body along with the views of the Advisory Board, if any, received in the meantime for the final consideration of the Governing Body.

---

#### Clause 14

It shall frame rules and regulations for the proper management and maintenance of all such properties as mentioned in Paragraph C (2)(i) above.

---

#### Clause 15

The Governing Body, if it thinks proper can entrust the duties of two or more Office-bearers of the Kendra Sangha to one person.

---

#### Clause 16

It can form different Committees / Sub-Committees consisting of suitable members of the Kendra Sangha for different purposes including the celebration of ceremonial functions.

---

#### Clause 17

As the guiding principles of the Kendra Sangha is to avoid all confrontations and dissensions amongst its members, all decisions taken by the Governing Body should normally be unanimous in all spiritual matters. Even then if in some matters, a division becomes unavoidable and if on such occasion the members are equally divided, the President of the Governing Body shall be competent to use his casting vote.

---

#### Clause 18

It can, as a temporary measure, arrange to take-over the seva and pujas of Shri Shri Thakur in any Sakha Sangha and Asana Mandir if it is convinced that serious omissions and commissions have been made resulting in serious departure from the ideologies of Shri Shri Thakur. Any expenditure incurred in this connection by the Kendra Sangha out of the sinking fund shall be reimbursed by the Sakha Sangha subsequently.

---

#### Clause 19

It can undertake any other activities, which will be felt necessary and helpful for fulfillment of the purpose of Shri Shri Thakur in establishing this Nilachala Saraswat Sangha by adopting a unanimous resolution in the Governing Body.
