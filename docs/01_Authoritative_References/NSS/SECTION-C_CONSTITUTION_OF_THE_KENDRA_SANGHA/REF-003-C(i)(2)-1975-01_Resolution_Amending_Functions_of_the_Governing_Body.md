### Constitutional Provision

**(FOUNDED BY NIGAMANANDA SARASWATI DEB IN 1934)**

**President:** Sri Chaitanya Charan Das, At./P.O. Swargadwar, Puri

**Secretary:** Sri Durga Charan Mohanty, At./P.O. Swargadwar, Puri

*(This letterhead block appears at the beginning of the Resolution section in the source, before the individual resolutions that follow — including this Resolution No. 1 and the companion Resolution No. 2, see `REF-003-C(i)(8)-1975-02`.)*

Following two sub-sections may be added after the sub section No. VI of the sub-chapter "Functions of the Governing Body" occurring in Chapter "C" of the Bye-law.

VI(A) The Governing Body shall have power to acquire, own and dispose off the properties in the name of Nilachala Saraswata Sangha and all such properties along with the existing properties shall vest in the Governing Body.

VI(B) The acquisition by purchase and disposal of properties by sale, if effected by documents shall be executed by the President, Secretary or Parichalak of the Sangha as resolved in the meeting of the Governing Body and these should be deemed to be part of the Bye-law from the date, the existing Bye-law came into force.

The above resolution has been passed unanimously in a meeting of the General Body of the Nilachala Saraswata Sangha, Puri, vide Regn. No. 7391/390 of 1973-74 held in the Office of the Sangha on 21-10-1975, in the afternoon.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "Copy of the Resolution No.1 dated 21-10-1975" with the two inserted sub-sections labeled VI(A) and VI(B), followed by an unlettered closing attestation paragraph, exactly as they appear in the original Resolution text.
