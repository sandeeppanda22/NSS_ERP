### Statutory Provision

Following 2 Sections may be added after the section 6 of sub-Chapter 5 and sub section 6 may be added after sub section I of sub-chapter No-5 occurring in the chapter "C" of the Bye-law.

A. Chapter (C) Sub-Chapter-5 "Sub Clause G" — Any complaint written or unwritten by or against any of the regular members of any recognised Sangha, pertaining to any action or activity of the member concerned may be referred to the Governing Body for their consideration and necessary action by the Secretary of the Kendra Sangha, if he so desired in the larger interest of the Sangha and that in consultation with the Parichalak.

B. Chapter 'C' Sub-chapter-5 Clause-VII — Parichalak is competent to discharge any of his duties entrusted to him either under the Bye-law or by any Resolution of the Governing Body, through an Authorised agent nominated by him.

Clause-VIII — In the absence of any provision in the Bye-law, relating to any matter or matters the decision taken by Parichalak, Shri Durga Charan Mohanty in such matters shall be final and the said decision shall be placed before the Governing Body for information in the next meeting held immediately after the said decision.

The above resolution has been passed unanimously in a meeting of the General Body of the Nilachala Saraswata Sangha, Puri, vide Regn. No. 7391/390 of 1973-74 held in the Office of the Sangha on 21-10-1975, in the afternoon.

Signature of the 3 members of the Executive Body Attesting the copy of the Resolution.

Sd./- Chaitanya Charan Das

President

Sd./- Durga Charan Mohanty

Secretary

Sd./- Raghunath Pati

Asst. Secretary

*(This signature block appears after both 1975 Resolutions, at the very end of the Resolution section — following this Resolution No. 2 and the preceding Resolution No. 1, see `REF-003-C(i)(2)-1975-01`.)*

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "Copy of the Resolution No-2 dated 21-10-75" with the inserted sections labeled A and B, followed by an unlettered "Clause-VIII" and closing attestation paragraph, exactly as they appear in the original Resolution text.
