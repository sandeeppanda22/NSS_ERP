# REF-003-D — Advisory Board

## Subject

Constitution of the Advisory Board

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-003

---

### Constitutional Provision

**D. (a) Constitution of the Advisory Board:**

The Advisory Board of the Kendra Sangha shall be constituted by taking one representative from each of the branch organisation (Sakha Sanghas) who shall be elected by the Sakha Sanghas in the manner prescribed for the election of the Office bearers of the concerned Sakha Sangha.

The Advisory Board shall have (i) a President and (ii) a Secretary who shall be elected from amongst its members.

The term of the Advisory Board shall be for three years from the date of function as such.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "D. (a) Constitution of the Advisory Board" as three unlettered narrative paragraphs, not subdivided into further clauses in the official Bye-Law.

## Subject

Duties of the Advisory Board

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-003

---

### Constitutional Provision

**(b) Duties of the Advisory Board:**

(i) The Board shall meet at least four times in a year at the suitable places to be decided by the President of the Board in consultation with the President of the Governing Body to discuss various affairs of the Kendra Sangha with particular reference to the implementation of the decision of the Governing Body in all matters and shall advise the Governing Body accordingly. But if any reference is made by the Governing Body in accordance with the provisions of Para (C)(2)(xiii), the President shall take immediate steps for convening a meeting of the Advisory Board for the consideration of the same along with other items, if any, in the agenda and submit their considered views to the Governing Body at their earliest possible opportunity.

(ii) The meetings of the Advisory Board shall be convened by the Secretary of the Board and agenda of the meeting should be framed in consultation with the President of the Board.

(iii) The Advisory Board may constitute committees, sub-committees to look into the specific affairs of the Kendra Sangha.

(iv) The Advisory Board shall arrange to submit the reports of the Committee/Sub-Committees and all the proceedings of the meetings of the Board to the Governing Body.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "(b) Duties of the Advisory Board" with sub-items numbered (i) through (iv), exactly as they appear in the official Bye-Law.

## Subject

Duties of the President of the Advisory Board

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-003

---

### Constitutional Provision

**(c) Duties of the President of the Advisory Board:** All meetings of the Board shall be presided over by the President of the Board, but in a combined meeting of the Governing Body and the Advisory Board, the President of the Governing Body will preside over the same.

The above provision is preserved in the source under the heading "(c) Duties of the President of the Advisory Board" as a single unlettered statement, not subdivided into further clauses in the official Bye-Law.

---

## Subject

Duties of the Secretary of the Advisory Board

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-003

---

### Constitutional Provision

**(d) Duties of the Secretary of the Advisory Board:**

(i) The Secretary of the Advisory Board shall convene the meeting of the Advisory Board and frame agenda for the meetings in consultation with the President of the Board.

(ii) He shall arrange to submit the proceedings of all meetings of the Board to the Governing Body.

(iii) He shall also look to the proper implementation of the decisions of the Advisory Board.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "(d) Duties of the Secretary of the Advisory Board" with sub-items numbered (i) through (iii), exactly as they appear in the official Bye-Law.

**Remarks**

The cross-reference to "Para (C)(2)(xiii)" in the Duties of the Advisory Board refers to the annual budget provision in REF-003-C(i)(2) (Functions of the Governing Body, Clause (xiii)), which requires draft budgets and financially significant draft resolutions to be referred to the Advisory Board for considered views before final Governing Body consideration. This cross-reference is preserved verbatim from the official Bye-Law and shall not be reinterpreted, and has been confirmed against both the source PDF and the source docx.
