### Constitutional Provision

**[c] Utilisation of the Funds:**

(i) The funds of the Kendra Sangha shall be utilised as per the decisions of the Governing Body.

(ii) The funds shall only be spent for the management of the Kendra Sangha, Nilachala Kutir, Smruti Mandir at Puri, the Sikshya Kendra at Biratung, different Sakha Sanghas affiliated to the Kendra Sangha or any other institutions run or sponsored by the Kendra Sangha.

(iii) Necessary expenditures to be incurred for the performance of 'pancha yagna' of which 'Atithi Satkar' (entertainment of guests) will be performed mainly at Sikshya Kendra.

(iv) Any funds raised or donations received for any specific purpose shall be spent and utilised for that particular purpose only.

(v) The Governing Body can sanction any amount adopting due procedures as donations to the Saraswata Matha and the Guru Dhama for specific purpose.

(vi) The Secretary or the Assistant Secretary shall sue and be sued on behalf of the Kendra Sangha and any one of them will represent the Kendra Sangha in the offices of the Government. Local bodies and other authorities can receive donations and grant on behalf of the Kendra Sangha with prior approval of the Governing Body.

The above provisions are preserved in the source under the heading "[c] Utilisation of the Funds" with sub-items numbered (i) through (vi), exactly as they appear in the official Bye-Law.
