# NSS ERP — Audit Business Rules

**Document ID:** SOL-AUDIT-003
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** DRAFT — SOURCE ALIGNED
**Module:** Audit
**Parent System:** Nilachala Saraswata Sangha ERP

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the business rules for the centralized Audit Module.

The current Audit foundation consists of:

    audit_master
    system_event_log

The Audit Module provides centralized traceability for significant system
actions and historical changes.

---

# 2. Rule Status

Each rule is classified as:

- FROZEN — established by the current project source
- SOURCE-ALIGNED — directly supported by existing project standards/rules
- PENDING — requires further design/approval
- FUTURE — outside the current frozen scope

---

# 3. Core Audit Rules

## AUD-BR-001 — Centralized Audit

**Status:** FROZEN

The NSS ERP shall maintain a centralized Audit capability.

The current Audit foundation consists of:

    audit_master
    system_event_log

No parallel audit mechanism shall be created by individual business
modules unless specifically approved.

---

## AUD-BR-002 — Cross-Module Audit

**Status:** FROZEN

Audit is a cross-cutting capability.

Business modules shall use the common Audit framework for actions that require
centralized auditability.

---

## AUD-BR-003 — Significant Actions Must Be Traceable

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Significant system actions shall remain traceable.

Examples include:

    Status changes
    Enrollment
    Event actions
    Administrative corrections
    Approval-related actions
    Other significant business changes

The exact audit coverage is determined by the relevant business module and
common audit standards.

---

# 4. Historical Preservation

## AUD-BR-004 — History Never Deleted

**Status:** FROZEN

Audit history shall be preserved.

The NSS ERP principle is:

    History Never Deleted

Historical audit records shall not be physically deleted during normal
business operations.

---

## AUD-BR-005 — Physical Audit Deletion Prohibited

**Status:** FROZEN

Normal users and normal business workflows shall not physically delete
audit history.

---

## AUD-BR-006 — Business Record Deletion Does Not Delete Audit History

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Deactivation or soft deletion of a business record shall not remove the
corresponding audit history.

Conceptually:

    Business Record
        ↓
    Deactivated

does not mean:

    Audit History
        ↓
    Deleted

---

# 5. Audit vs Business History

## AUD-BR-007 — Separate Responsibilities

**Status:** FROZEN

Business-domain history and centralized audit history are separate concepts.

Business history answers:

    What happened to the business entity?

Audit history answers:

    What auditable system action occurred?

---

## AUD-BR-008 — Domain History Remains Authoritative

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Where a module has a domain-specific history entity, the domain-specific
history remains the authoritative business history.

The Audit Module does not replace it.

---

# 6. Actor Accountability

## AUD-BR-009 — Actor Traceability

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Where an auditable action is performed by an authenticated user, the action
shall be attributable to that user through the existing identity and
authentication architecture.

---

## AUD-BR-010 — No Duplicate User Identity

**Status:** FROZEN

The Audit Module shall not create a separate user identity system.

It shall reuse the existing:

    Authentication
    Person
    Membership
    Sangha Sevi

identity architecture where applicable.

---

## AUD-BR-011 — System-Generated Actions

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Where an auditable action is generated by the system rather than directly by
a human user, the audit record shall be attributable to the system process
according to the final technical design.

The Audit Module shall distinguish system-generated activity from
human-generated activity where required.

---

# 7. Action Traceability

## AUD-BR-012 — Auditable Action

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

An audit event should provide sufficient information to establish:

    What action occurred
    When it occurred
    Who or what caused it
    What business context was affected

The exact physical representation is a table-design concern.

---

## AUD-BR-013 — Target Traceability

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Where necessary, an audit event shall identify the relevant business
context/entity/record.

The final representation of target identification is not frozen by this
document.

---

## AUD-BR-014 — No Unsupported Polymorphic FK

**Status:** FROZEN

The Audit Module shall not assume a polymorphic foreign-key structure unless
that architecture is explicitly approved.

---

# 8. Audit Timing

## AUD-BR-015 — Event Timestamp

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

An auditable event shall record when the event occurred or was recorded,
according to the final timestamp standard.

---

## AUD-BR-016 — Historical Ordering

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Audit events shall retain sufficient temporal information to reconstruct the
sequence of significant actions.

---

# 9. Status Changes

## AUD-BR-017 — Status Changes Are Auditable

**Status:** FROZEN

Status changes requiring auditability shall be recorded.

This principle is explicitly applied in existing module rules, including
Sevak status changes.

---

## AUD-BR-018 — Original State Preservation

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Where an auditable correction changes a value or status, the original state
must remain historically traceable.

The audit trail shall not erase the previous state.

---

# 10. Corrections

## AUD-BR-019 — Post-Completion Corrections

**Status:** FROZEN

Where a business record is locked after completion, corrections shall follow
the applicable centralized correction/audit mechanism.

---

## AUD-BR-020 — Correction Traceability

**Status:** FROZEN

A post-completion correction shall preserve, where required:

    Original Value
    Requested Value
    Reason
    Requested By
    Request Timestamp
    Approver(s)
    Approval Timestamp
    Final Change
    Audit Trail

Existing Sevak rules explicitly establish these requirements for
post-completion correction.

---

## AUD-BR-021 — Original Value Never Disappears

**Status:** FROZEN

A correction shall not silently overwrite historical truth.

The original value must remain recoverable through the appropriate business
history/audit mechanism.

---

## AUD-BR-022 — Requester Cannot Approve Own Correction

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Where the applicable approval workflow requires separation of requester and
approver, the requester shall not approve their own correction.

This rule is already established in the existing post-completion correction
model.

---

# 11. Audit and Approval

## AUD-BR-023 — Approval Actions Auditable

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Where an approval action is designated as auditable, the action shall be
traceable.

The approval workflow itself remains owned by the Applications/Approvals
domain.

---

## AUD-BR-024 — Audit Does Not Own Approval

**Status:** FROZEN

The Audit Module shall not become the authoritative owner of approval
decisions.

It records the auditable activity.

---

# 12. Audit and RBAC

## AUD-BR-025 — Existing RBAC

**Status:** FROZEN

The Audit Module shall use the existing NSS ERP RBAC framework.

No separate Audit authorization architecture shall be introduced.

---

## AUD-BR-026 — Audit Access Restricted

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Access to centralized audit history shall be restricted to appropriately
authorized users.

Ordinary users shall not automatically receive unrestricted audit access.

---

## AUD-BR-027 — Audit Access Is Itself Sensitive

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Because audit records may expose sensitive operational information, access
to audit history shall follow the common security and organizational-scope
rules.

---

# 13. Audit Integrity

## AUD-BR-028 — Audit Records Are Historical Evidence

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Audit records shall be treated as historical evidence of system activity.

Normal workflows shall not modify them casually.

---

## AUD-BR-029 — No Silent Audit Modification

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Audit records shall not be silently altered.

Any exceptional administrative correction of audit data must itself be
controlled and auditable.

---

## AUD-BR-030 — Audit Correction Is Exceptional

**Status:** PENDING

The exact process for correcting an erroneous audit record is not frozen by
the current source.

A dedicated correction process may be defined later if required.

---

# 14. Audit and Business Modules

## AUD-BR-031 — Membership

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Membership actions that require auditability shall be recorded through the
common Audit framework.

Examples include:

    Approval
    Status change
    Transfer
    Renewal
    Reinstatement
    Administrative correction

The Membership Module remains the business owner.

---

## AUD-BR-032 — Organization

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Significant organization changes requiring auditability shall use the
central Audit framework.

---

## AUD-BR-033 — Governance

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Significant governance actions requiring auditability shall use the common
Audit framework.

Examples may include:

    Position assignment
    Acting assignment
    Governance changes
    Approval decisions

The Governance Module remains the business owner.

---

## AUD-BR-034 — Attendance

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Attendance corrections and other auditable attendance actions shall use the
central Audit framework where required by Attendance rules.

---

## AUD-BR-035 — Sevak

**Status:** FROZEN

Sevak status changes, enrollment and event actions require auditability.

The existing Sevak rules explicitly state:

    Auditability required for all status changes, enrollment, and event
    actions.

---

## AUD-BR-036 — UPBS

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

UPBS operations requiring auditability shall use the common Audit framework.

Examples may include:

    Registration changes
    Event changes
    Accommodation changes
    Administrative corrections

UPBS remains the business owner.

---

## AUD-BR-037 — Finance

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Financial actions requiring auditability shall use the common Audit
framework.

Finance remains the authoritative financial domain.

---

# 15. Event Lifecycle Audit

## AUD-BR-038 — Event Cancellation

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Where an event cancellation is an auditable action, the cancellation shall be
recorded.

Existing Sevak event rules explicitly require cancellation to be audited.

---

## AUD-BR-039 — Event Rescheduling

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Where an event is rescheduled, the relevant history and audit trail shall be
preserved.

Existing event rules require complete rescheduling history and auditability
for post-start changes.

---

## AUD-BR-040 — Post-Start Event Changes

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

For auditable post-start event changes, the system shall preserve relevant:

    Original Status
    Changed Status
    Change Date/Time
    Changed By
    Reason/Remarks
    Previous Date/Time

This is explicitly established in the existing Sevak event rules.

---

# 16. Completion and Closure

## AUD-BR-041 — Completion History

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Completion of an event or workflow shall not destroy its audit history.

Existing event rules require the audit trail to remain after completion.

---

## AUD-BR-042 — Post-Completion Lock

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Where a domain locks records after completion, subsequent changes must use
the applicable correction and audit process.

---

# 17. Audit and Notifications

## AUD-BR-043 — Independent Responsibilities

**Status:** FROZEN

Audit and Notification are separate concerns.

A business action may produce:

    Audit Event

and:

    Notification

without one being the other.

---

## AUD-BR-044 — No Duplicate Notification Engine

**Status:** FROZEN

The Audit Module shall not create a separate notification system.

---

# 18. Audit and Application Logs

## AUD-BR-045 — Audit Is Not Debug Logging

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

The Audit Module is not a replacement for technical application logs.

Application logs may support:

    Debugging
    Error diagnosis
    Performance analysis

Audit records support:

    Accountability
    Historical traceability
    Governance
    Business review

---

## AUD-BR-046 — Errors Are Not Automatically Audit Events

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

A technical error shall not automatically become an authoritative business
audit event.

Only events requiring auditability shall be recorded as Audit history.

---

# 19. Audit and Soft Delete

## AUD-BR-047 — Soft Delete Preservation

**Status:** FROZEN

When a business record is soft-deleted:

    is_active = FALSE

the corresponding audit history remains preserved.

---

# 20. Audit and Security

## AUD-BR-048 — Security Event Traceability

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Security-sensitive operations may require audit records.

The Authentication & Security Module owns the security business rules.

The Audit Module provides centralized traceability.

---

# 21. Audit and Identity

## AUD-BR-049 — Existing Identity Model

**Status:** FROZEN

Audit shall reuse the existing identity architecture.

No new permanent identity shall be created for audit actors.

---

## AUD-BR-050 — Person vs Actor

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

A Person is a domain identity.

An audit actor is the user/system context responsible for an auditable action.

The two concepts shall not be conflated.

---

# 22. Audit Master Rules

## AUD-BR-051 — `audit_master`

**Status:** FROZEN

`audit_master` is part of the frozen Audit foundation.

Its exact business purpose and controlled values require the detailed table
design.

---

## AUD-BR-052 — No Unapproved Audit Masters

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

New audit master categories or controlled values shall not be introduced
without an approved requirement.

---

# 23. System Event Log Rules

## AUD-BR-053 — `system_event_log`

**Status:** FROZEN

`system_event_log` is part of the frozen Audit foundation.

It provides the centralized system-event history mechanism.

---

## AUD-BR-054 — Event History Preservation

**Status:** FROZEN

System events recorded as authoritative audit history shall remain
historically available.

---

## AUD-BR-055 — Event Identity

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Each recorded system event must be uniquely identifiable.

The exact identifier and physical representation are defined by the table
design.

---

# 24. Audit Event Catalogue

## AUD-BR-056 — Controlled Event Catalogue

**Status:** PENDING

The project has not yet frozen a complete centralized list of audit event
types.

Therefore this document does not freeze a final enumeration such as:

    CREATE
    UPDATE
    DELETE
    LOGIN
    LOGOUT
    APPROVE
    REJECT
    EXPORT

These may be used as implementation concepts only after formal approval.

---

# 25. Field-Level Audit

## AUD-BR-057 — Field-Level Change Tracking

**Status:** PENDING

The current source does not establish a separate frozen field-level audit
table.

Therefore:

    audit_field_change

and similar structures are not part of the current Audit foundation.

If required later, they shall be separately designed and approved.

---

# 26. Login Audit

## AUD-BR-058 — Login History

**Status:** PENDING

The current Audit foundation does not freeze a dedicated login-history table.

Authentication/Security owns authentication events.

Any requirement for centralized login auditing must be separately designed.

---

# 27. Access Audit

## AUD-BR-059 — Access History

**Status:** PENDING

The current source does not freeze a separate access-audit entity.

No additional access-log table shall be created without approved requirements.

---

# 28. Approval Audit

## AUD-BR-060 — Approval History

**Status:** PENDING

Approval history remains owned by the Applications/Approvals framework.

The Audit Module may record significant approval actions, but no separate
`audit_approval_history` table is frozen.

---

# 29. Traceability

## AUD-BR-061 — End-to-End Traceability

**Status:** FROZEN

The NSS ERP project requires end-to-end traceability across:

    REF
      ↓
    REQ
      ↓
    SOLUTION
      ↓
    CODE
      ↓
    TEST
      ↓
    RELEASE

Audit and traceability are related but distinct governance concepts.

The project's traceability standard explicitly requires this lifecycle
chain.

---

## AUD-BR-062 — Audit Supports Traceability

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Audit records may provide evidence supporting implementation and operational
traceability.

Audit does not replace the project's formal requirement traceability
mechanism.

---

# 30. Historical Integrity

## AUD-BR-063 — Original History Preserved

**Status:** FROZEN

Historical information shall remain available for review.

This includes relevant:

    Original Values
    Previous Status
    Previous Date/Time
    Previous Assignments
    Correction History
    Audit Trail

where required by the originating business rule.

---

# 31. Administrative Accountability

## AUD-BR-064 — Administrative Actions

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Administrative actions designated as auditable shall be recorded.

The exact administrative event catalogue belongs to the Administration and
Audit design.

---

# 32. Audit Visibility

## AUD-BR-065 — Role-Based Audit Visibility

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Audit visibility shall follow the common RBAC and organizational-scope
architecture.

A user may be permitted to:

    View audit history
    Search audit history
    Export audit history

only where authorized.

Exact permissions remain pending centralized RBAC design.

---

# 33. Audit Export

## AUD-BR-066 — Audit Export

**Status:** PENDING

The current source does not freeze a dedicated audit export requirement.

Any export capability must comply with common security, privacy, and
authorization rules.

---

# 34. Audit Retention

## AUD-BR-067 — Historical Retention

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Audit history is intended to remain part of the long-term historical record.

Exact technical archive/retention periods are not frozen by this document.

---

# 35. Backup

## AUD-BR-068 — Audit Included in Backup

**Status:** SOURCE-ALIGNED

Audit data shall be included in the normal database backup strategy.

The Backup & Technical Module owns backup and restore mechanisms.

Audit does not create a separate backup system.

---

# 36. No Duplicate Frameworks

## AUD-BR-069 — Common Framework Reuse

**Status:** FROZEN

Audit shall reuse common enterprise frameworks for:

    Identity
    Authentication
    RBAC
    Notifications
    Approvals
    Documents
    Backup
    Organizational Scope

It shall not create duplicate versions of these capabilities.

---

    Significant system activity must remain attributable,
    traceable, and historically preservable.
