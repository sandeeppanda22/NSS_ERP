## Frozen Decisions

### Core Principle

Person ≠ Member

A Person may exist without Membership.

A Member must always be a Person.

---

### Person Identity

person_code

Examples:

P00000001

P00000002

P00000003

---

### Contact Rules

mobile_number

* UNIQUE
* NULL ALLOWED

email

* NOT UNIQUE
* NULL ALLOWED

At least one contact method required.

Valid:

* Mobile only
* Email only
* Mobile + Email

Invalid:

* Mobile = NULL
* Email = NULL

---

### Date of Birth

Date of Birth is optional for Person records.

Date of Birth becomes mandatory before Membership approval.

---

### Name Rules

first_name

* Mandatory

middle_name

* Optional

last_name

* Optional

---

### Gender Rules

gender_pk

* Mandatory

Managed through gender_master

---

### Address Rules

Multiple addresses supported.

Address types managed through address_type_master.

Examples:

* Permanent
* Current
* Official

---

### Membership Rules

Person records do not store:

* Sangha Sevi ID
* Membership Type
* Membership Status

These belong to the Membership Module.

---

### Authentication Decision

Every Login belongs to a Sangha Sevi.

Not every Person may log into the system.

Authentication implementation deferred until Membership and Authentication modules are designed.

---

### Document Management Decision

Person photos and identity documents are deferred.

Future Document Management Module will handle:

* Person Photo
* Aadhaar
* Passport
* Voter ID
